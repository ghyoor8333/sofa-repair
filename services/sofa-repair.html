A PRACTICAL ALTERNATIVE

Why Repair Your Sofa Instead of Replacing It?

A good sofa is built on a solid wooden frame, and that frame often outlasts the foam and fabric. Many sofas that look worn are structurally sound: the seat has flattened, a spring has snapped or a seam has opened, and those problems can often be fixed.

  • Spend less: A repair is often a fraction of the cost of replacing a comparable sofa.
  • Keep the style you like: Preserve the size, shape and design that already suit your home.
  • Get it back sooner: Most repairs are completed in a day or two, depending on the work.
  • Reduce waste: Repair keeps bulky furniture in use for longer.
  • Improve comfort: Upgrading compressed foam can make an old sofa feel supportive again.
WHAT WE REPAIR

Our Sofa Repair Services

From a loose joint to a tired seat, we inspect the cause and recommend the repair your sofa needs.

1. Sofa Frame and Joint Repair

Loose joints, cracked corners and wobbling legs can make a sofa unstable. We open the affected area, replace damaged sections and reinforce weak joints with wood, glue, screws or brackets. Minor repairs are done at home; if major structural work needs a workshop, we explain that before proceeding.

Common signs: creaking or cracking, a sofa that wobbles or tilts, visible cracks, or loose arms.

2. Foam Replacement and Seat Repair

Compressed foam can leave permanent dips, hard seats and slumping back cushions. We replace old foam with pieces cut to fit your sofa and offer different firmness options. A layer of wrapping fibre or dacron helps cushions look full and lets the cover sit smoothly.

Common signs: sunken seats, lumpy cushions, poor back support or difficulty getting up.

3. Sofa Spring Repair

A broken or loose spring can create an uneven seat, a hard spot or a spring that pushes through the fabric. We replace broken springs, re-tie loose ones and renew worn webbing or the support base to restore even seating.

Common signs: a twanging or squeaking sound, one side sitting lower, or a lump under the seat.

4. Sofa Cushion Repair

We refill flat cushions, replace foam inserts, repair zips and rebuild cushion shape. If a cover is beyond repair, we can make a new one using a suitable fabric from our range.

5. Stitching, Seam and Fabric Repair

We re-stitch opened seams, patch or replace damaged panels, and repair piping and trim. Fabric is matched as closely as possible. If wear affects the whole sofa, we can discuss full sofa upholstery.

6. Sofa Polish and Wooden Finish Touch-Up

Faded, scratched or dull wooden arms, legs and exposed frames can be sanded, polished and refinished. For more extensive work, visit our wooden sofa repair page.

7. Recliner, Sofa Chair and Sofa Cum Bed Repair

We repair sofa chairs and loungers, plus sofa cum bed mechanisms such as hinges, locks, folding frames and wheels. Learn more about sofa cum bed repair and sofa chair repair.

8. Leather and Leatherette Repair

Cracks, peeling, tears and fading can be addressed with stitching, patching, foam correction and colour touch-up. See our leather sofa repair service for details.

A STRAIGHTFORWARD GUIDE

Repair or Replace? How to Decide

Repair is usually a good choice when:

  • The frame is solid or has only minor damage.
  • The issue is sagging, a tear, a loose joint or worn fabric.
  • You like the sofa's design, size and fit in your home.
  • The sofa is a well-made piece worth keeping.

Consider replacement when:

  • The frame is badly rotted or extensively damaged by termites.
  • You never found the sofa comfortable or liked its shape.
  • The repair cost would be close to a comparable new sofa.

We will tell you honestly if a sofa is not worth repairing. That is part of the free inspection.

BETTER EVERYDAY CARE

Tips to Make Your Sofa Last Longer

  • Rotate and flip cushions every few weeks to spread wear evenly.
  • Discourage jumping on the sofa or sitting on its arms.
  • Keep it away from direct sunlight, which can fade fabric and dry leather.
  • Vacuum regularly to remove dust and grit that wear fabric.
  • Blot spills quickly rather than rubbing them.
  • Keep the sofa a little away from walls and heaters.
  • Fix small issues early; a loose joint or open seam can worsen over time.
