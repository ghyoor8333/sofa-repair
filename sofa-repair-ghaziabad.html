SOFA CARE AT HOME

Repair options for common sofa problems

We assess the frame, support, cushioning and covering before recommending a repair. The work required and whether it can be completed at home depends on the sofa's condition and parts needed.

Frame, springs and joints

Loose joints, unstable arms, broken supports and noisy or damaged springs can affect both safety and comfort. An inspection helps identify the source before repair is recommended.

Foam and cushion support

Sagging seats or uneven cushions may need foam replacement, support adjustment or cushion repair. The suitable option depends on the existing filling and construction.

Fabric, leather and stitching

Worn upholstery, open seams, small tears and faded coverings can be assessed for stitching, patch repair or a more complete re-covering.
